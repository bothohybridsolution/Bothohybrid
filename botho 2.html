How we work

We run as a remote-first company. You deal with one coordinator while approved teams do the work on site.

  1. Request

    Send your details by form or WhatsApp.

  2. Quote

    We confirm scope and a fixed price within one working day.

  3. Assign

    A vetted team is matched to your area and schedule.

  4. Clean

    The team arrives uniformed and works from a checklist.

  5. Check

    Photos and a sign-off are logged before the job closes.

Uniform design
